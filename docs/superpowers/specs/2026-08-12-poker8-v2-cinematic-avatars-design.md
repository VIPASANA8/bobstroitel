# Poker8 v2 Cinematic Table — Design

## Goal

Bring the mobile table's wood rail, deep felt, card presentation, chip clusters, betting markers, and player plaques close to the approved dark neon poker-room reference. Avatar portraits are explicitly deferred; the current avatar slot stays neutral and ready for future profile avatars.

## Scope

The work is limited to the mobile six-max game scene at widths up to 780 px. It preserves the poker engine, state updates, API behavior, seat assignment, desktop layout, current action controls, and the visual-only chat button.

No portrait or avatar assets are created in this pass. The reference supplies composition, material treatment, and information hierarchy; it does not authorize copying its depicted character identities or artwork.

## Visual System

The table becomes a cinematic scene with three depth layers:

1. **Room and table:** low-light wood grain outside a deep emerald felt, with a layered brown wood rail and paired green neon lines.
2. **Players:** each seat combines a neutral dark avatar medallion, two angled card backs behind it, and a compact black name/stack plaque beneath it. Accent colors are stable per visual seat: green at top, purple/cyan on the left, amber/magenta on the right, and electric blue for the local player.
3. **Game state:** community cards, pot, chip clusters, dealer button, wagers, turn state, and action tray stay data-driven from the existing renderer. The visual pass only changes their presentation.

## Asset and Fallback Contract

The avatar container keeps a stable size, glow, and crop. A future profile avatar must be able to override its background through `--profile-avatar-image` (or equivalent), without changing seat HTML, positioning, card backs, plaque styling, or responsive rules.

Until profile-image data exists in the API, the neutral avatar medallion remains visible with existing text initials for accessibility and identity. Card backs are CSS-rendered; no new raster assets are needed.

## Implementation Boundaries

Use a new additive visual pass after `v037`; do not rewrite prior visual passes. The pass may add presentation-only layers to existing seats, board, chip, and avatar wrappers. It must not add click handlers, fetch calls, persistence changes, or game-state mutations.

Card backs, chips, wood, and felt are CSS-rendered. This keeps mobile weight low and lets all dynamic card/chip values remain generated by the application.

## Responsive and State Rules

- At 360 × 800 and 402 × 874, neutral avatar, card-back, plaque, pot, board, dealer button, wagers, chip clusters, and actions remain visible without overlap.
- Folded, all-in, active-turn, pre-hand, and showdown styles continue using existing state classes.
- A neutral avatar cannot cover readable player name, stack, position, status, or player cards.
- At widths above 780 px, no cinematic avatar or fallback style is applied.
- When the viewport crosses the mobile breakpoint, the pass initializes its fallback visuals just as the existing six-max layer does.

## Verification

Verification will cover static loader contract, lack of new click/network/game-state behavior, focused regression tests, and browser inspection at 360 × 800, 402 × 874, desktop width, and desktop-to-mobile resize.

## Out of Scope

- generating temporary portrait assets;
- profile avatar upload, storage, API schema, or moderation;
- changing player identities or bot behavior;
- functional chat;
- copying the reference artwork or its depicted character identities;
- desktop-table redesign.
